What we're communicating

Nébula's launch requires two parallel communication tracks — internal alignment across Andino Labs globally, and external positioning in the LATAM (and future US) market.

Month 1 · Weeks 1–4

Ignite

Announce internally. Share the vision with all of Andino Labs. Brief C-Level and technical leaders. Begin external teaser presence.

Month 2 · Weeks 5–8

Engage

Deepen internal understanding through working sessions. Launch external thought leadership. Activate LinkedIn and specialized media outreach.

Month 3 · Weeks 9–12

Amplify

First external results and case stories. Periodic C-Level review. Expand media reach. Consolidate internal flywheel practices.

🏛️

Internal Objective

Every person at Andino Labs — LATAM and India — understands what Nébula is, why it matters strategically, and how it relates to their role. C-Level and technical leaders are active champions, not just informed stakeholders.

📡

External Objective

Andino Labs is recognized in LATAM as the AI engineering transformation partner with a proprietary platform. Target buyers (CTOs, VPs Engineering) see Nébula as a credible differentiator, not just a marketing claim.

Core Messages
Message 1 — What Nébula is
Nébula is Andino Labs' proprietary multi-agent, multi-LLM platform that covers the full AI Software Development Lifecycle — from intent capture through deployment — deployed inside client infrastructure, exclusive to Andino Labs engagements.
Message 2 — Why it matters
Most teams adopt AI tools individually and plateau. Nébula makes AI adoption a team capability, not a personal skill — with a feedback flywheel that compounds over time. It's the execution layer that turns AI intent into AI-ready engineering teams.
Message 3 — Why Andino Labs
Andino Labs brings 15+ years of digital engineering depth from one of the world's largest retail tech ecosystems (Bangalore) combined with LATAM-native delivery. No other partner in the region combines proprietary AI tooling with this level of engineering maturity.
Message 4 — The FINOS anchor
FINOS defines what an AI-ready organization needs. Andino Labs builds how to get there. Nébula is the infrastructure that operationalizes all 9 dimensions of AI readiness — from Technology and Data to Governance and Monitoring.

Executive alignment is not a one-time briefing — it's a recurring practice that keeps Nébula as the company's stated strategic priority and surfaces blockers early.

🎯

C-Level (CEO, CTO, COO, VP Sales) across LATAM and India HQ are not just aware of Nébula — they are active sponsors who communicate its priority publicly, allocate resources, and remove blockers.

3-Month Executive Arc
Month 1 — Align

Strategic Commitment

C-Level formally endorses Nébula as strategic priority #1 for the next 12 months. Budget, headcount, and marketing resources confirmed. Each executive commits to 2 public LinkedIn posts about Nébula in Month 1.

Month 2 — Activate

First Results Review

Review first internal deployment data, first pipeline deals where Nébula was positioned, and first market content performance. Identify gaps and adjust resourcing. C-Level approved quotes for media use.

Month 3 — Validate

Market Proof Points

Review first external validation signals: inbound interest, media coverage, conference speaking invitations. Decide on 6-month roadmap for Nébula positioning in LATAM and US expansion narrative.

Key Principle
The C-Level session is protected time — it is never canceled, never shortened, and always has a pre-read. Its regularity signals internally that Nébula is a real priority, not a project that will fade when the next thing gets attention.
